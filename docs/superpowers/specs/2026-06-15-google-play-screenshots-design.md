# MiLingo Google Play Screenshots Design

## Goal

Create six portrait Google Play screenshots that show the real MiLingo interface
clearly while using Limabo mascot poses to build a memorable visual identity.

## Output

- Six PNG images, each 1080 x 1920 pixels.
- Real app screenshots remain unchanged and readable.
- Each image uses one short Vietnamese headline with at most two lines.
- Important content stays inside safe margins.

## Visual Direction

- Use a clean promotional layout inspired by Gmail's Play Store screenshots.
- Place headline and one feature-specific Limabo pose in the upper area.
- Enlarge the real app screenshot to fill roughly 65-75% of image height.
- Let the screenshot extend toward the bottom edge.
- Do not place the screenshot inside a large white card or phone frame.
- Use a soft light background color that changes slightly per feature.
- Keep MiLingo orange as the primary accent.

## Composition

- Logo appears small near the top edge.
- Headline is the largest element in the upper area.
- Mascot complements the headline without covering it.
- Screenshot is centered and significantly larger than the earlier preview.
- Crop only empty or low-value screenshot regions. Never crop core feature UI.
- Avoid extra descriptive paragraphs unless needed for clarity.

## Content Rules

- Do not invent app features or alter real interface content.
- Do not use rankings, awards, pricing, reviews, or download claims.
- Avoid excessive decorative elements and promotional text.
- Preserve Vietnamese spelling and diacritics exactly.

## First Review Asset

Create a revised Home screenshot first. It must use the waving Limabo pose,
headline `Học mỗi ngày cùng Limabo`, and a larger interface extending toward the
bottom edge. After approval, apply the same system to the remaining five images.
